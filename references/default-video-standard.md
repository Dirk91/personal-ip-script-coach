# Default Video Standard

Use this standard only as a fallback. The binding order is:

> 用户当前指令 > 用户提供的标准 > 本默认标准 > Agent 一般判断

When a user supplies a standard, obey it and use this file to只补充未覆盖部分. A conflict is resolved upward, never by averaging incompatible rules.

## 1. Pass the audience's first three questions

Before polishing language, make sure a target viewer can answer:

1. **这件事跟我有关吗？** The opening names or shows a recognizable person, condition, desire, or situation.
2. **我能马上听懂吗？** The subject, tension, and promise use concrete language rather than insider shorthand.
3. **我看完能拿走什么？** The viewer can anticipate a judgment, method, path, warning, or new understanding.

## 2. Choose one primary objective

Each video has **一个主要目标**: follow, consult, save, comment, share, buy, or another explicitly stated result. Secondary effects may occur, but they must not compete with the main action.

The ending, amount of explanation, and proof should serve that objective. A consultation video needs qualification and trust; a save-oriented video needs reusable steps; a share-oriented video needs a clear social reason to pass it on.

## 3. Put relevance and pain near the opening

Pain should be visible early enough for the right viewer to recognize themselves. Prefer a specific situation, repeated behavior, cost, stuck moment, or contrast over a broad label such as “迷茫” or “没有前途”.

Pain placement does not mean fear bait. Lead with the truth that matters, not the most frightening sentence available.

## 4. Replace claims with visible evidence

Important claims need **可见证据** whenever the creator has it:

- a real case or message;
- a before-and-after result;
- a personal event or work experience;
- an action, object, number, dialogue, or scene;
- a repeated observation with its boundary stated.

Use events to carry emotion. “She closed the laptop and sat in the car for twenty minutes” is more usable than “she felt defeated” when that event is true.

## 5. Match the size of the promise

Decide whether the video solves one small point or maps a complete subject. Do not use a broad title for a single anecdote, and do not bury a sharp insight under a miniature course.

For a small point, show one problem, one mechanism, and one next action. For a complete subject, provide an explicit map and make the scope credible for the available time.

## 6. Match form to interaction goal

| Goal | Useful form |
|---|---|
| Follow | A recognizable worldview plus a reason to expect more |
| Consult | A qualified problem, credible proof, and clear fit |
| Save | Steps, checks, or a reusable decision rule |
| Comment | A real choice, tension, or experience worth answering |
| Share | Language that helps the viewer explain something to another person |
| Convert | Clear fit, outcome boundary, proof, and next action |

Do not attach the same generic closing line to every goal.

## 7. Build trust before pressure

Use precise boundaries, honest uncertainty, and relevant evidence. Do not exaggerate risk, promise a guaranteed outcome, invent scarcity, or imply that one path fits everyone.

This standard does not guarantee virality. “爆款” is an ambition, not a claim the Skill can verify in advance.
