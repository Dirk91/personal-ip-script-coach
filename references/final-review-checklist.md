# Final Review Checklist

Run this gate before presenting a manuscript as complete. Judge confirmed material, not what the Agent could hypothetically add.

## Content and audience

- **目标观众**: Can the intended person recognize themselves near the start?
- **内容目标**: Is there one primary result for this video?
- **痛点位置**: Does a visible problem or desire arrive early enough?
- **核心观点**: Can the final claim be stated in one clear sentence without overstating it?

## Evidence and movement

- **真实案例**: Is there a relevant person, event, message, project, or observation?
- **个人证据**: Is it clear why this creator can credibly speak?
- **具体情绪**: Are important feelings carried by real actions, scenes, objects, dialogue, or numbers where available?
- **因果与行动路径**: Does the script explain what changed, why it mattered, and what the viewer can do?

## Voice and truth

- **原话与语感**: Does it still sound like the creator rather than a generic polished narrator?
- **事实**: Are experiences, identities, results, data, dialogue, and motives confirmed or clearly marked for verification?
- Are repeated AI-shaped contrasts, symmetrical slogans, and abstract summaries removed when they erase the person's wording?

## Closing and offer

- **CTA**: Does the final action match the primary content goal and feel earned by the preceding material?
- **产品分层**: If follow, course, service, and one-to-one help appear, is it clear who each is for without forcing every option into the ending?

## Privacy and output safety

- **隐私**: Are names, avatars, handles, contact details, private messages, and unrelated metadata removed or approved for use?
- **安全输出**: With file access, is the work saved to a new relative path unless overwrite was explicitly requested? Without file access, does the response avoid claiming a file was written?

## Readiness decision

After reviewing every item,只能返回一个 status:

- `ready` — the script can be delivered as complete and contains no unresolved critical fact or structural gap.
- `ready with noted gaps` — the script is usable, but explicitly listed non-critical facts or options still need confirmation.
- `not ready—continue interview` — a missing goal, audience, evidence, mechanism, or truth/privacy issue prevents responsible completion.

If the user explicitly requests an early draft, label it provisional; do not misuse a readiness status to imply completion.
